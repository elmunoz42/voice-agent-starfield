## How Starborn Powers Work

Starborn powers are supernatural abilities gained in ancient temples. You get your first power, Anti-Gravity Field, during Into the Unknown. More temples appear as you progress through the main quest and collect Artifacts. Your scanner shows a gravitational anomaly in the system. Fly into it, land on the planet, find the temple and catch the floating lights inside (a zero-g chase) to gain the power.

There are 24 powers. Temple locations are largely randomized per playthrough, so planet guides from other players may not match yours. Use the anomalies in your own game.

Powers use a Power meter that regenerates over time. Each power has a cost (15 to 45). Free Lanes (2026) lets you upgrade powers with Quantum Essence, which drops from destroyed Starborn ships and defeated Starborn enemies. Quantum Essence can be bound to Favorites.

Powers grow stronger by revisiting the temples in New Game Plus.

## Starborn Powers List (Combat)

- Anti-Gravity Field (45): lifts enemies into the air, where they are helpless briefly. Temple Eta.
- Gravity Wave (25): a gravity pulse that knocks enemies back. Temple Beta.
- Gravity Well (45): traps and crushes nearby enemies. Temple Delta.
- Earthbound (15): sets local gravity to Earth's, making enemies fall. Temple Epsilon.
- Grav Dash (15): launches you forward and briefly boosts damage. Temple Zeta.
- Particle Beam (15): fires a damaging beam. Temple Omega.
- Solar Flare (25): sets enemies on fire. Temple Theta.
- Sunless Space (35): fires ice that freezes living things. Temple Kappa.
- Supernova (45): an area explosion around you. Temple Xi.
- Create Vacuum (25): pulls oxygen out of enemies over time. Temple Gamma.
- Life Forced (25): drains an enemy's health into yours. Temple Pi.
- Inner Demon (35): makes an enemy turn against its allies. Temple Upsilon.

## Starborn Powers List (Utility and Defense)

- Phased Time (45): slows time briefly. From the Buried Temple, gained late in the story. Many players rank it the best power.
- Reactive Shield (35): a shield that reflects attacks and raises damage resistance. Temple Tau. A top pick for survivability.
- Moon Form (35): roots you in place with very high damage resistance. Temple Rho.
- Void Form (45): makes you invisible briefly. Temple Omicron.
- Parallel Self (45): summons a duplicate of you to fight. Temple Sigma.
- Creators' Peace (25): nearby enemies stop using their weapons for a while. Temple Nu. Underrated.
- Alien Reanimation (35): revives a dead alien creature to fight for you. Temple Phi.
- Personal Atmosphere (45): creates breathable oxygen around you. Temple Alpha.
- Precognition (35): shows an NPC's path or how they would react to a dialogue choice. Temple Iota.
- Sense Star Stuff (15): see nearby living creatures through walls. Temple Chi.
- Elemental Pull (25): pulls nearby resources toward you. Temple Psi. Great for mining.
- Eternal Harvest (25): regrows nearby plants for harvesting. Temple Lambda.

## New Game Plus: What Carries Over

When you enter the Unity and cross into a new universe, you start New Game Plus.

You keep: your level, skills and Starborn powers. Powers can rank up again by revisiting temples in each new universe.

You lose: items, credits, ships, outposts, companions and relationships. The new universe resets them, and characters won't know you.

You gain: the Starborn Guardian ship and a Starborn spacesuit, which improve with each NG+ cycle.

Quantum Entanglement Device (Free Lanes, 2026): lets you carry some of your items through the Unity, easing the loss of your inventory.

Universes can differ. Some cycles have changes, such as different versions of events or characters, or a universe where something unusual happened. You can also skip the main quest in NG+ and go straight to the Artifacts, or play it again with new dialogue options as a Starborn.

## Outpost Building Overview

Outposts allow you to extract resources, build bases, and establish production chains across the Settled Systems.

### Getting Started

**Requirements**:
- Outpost Beacon (found in your inventory under Equipment)
- A planet with resources you want to extract
- Credits for building materials

**Choosing a Location**:
1. Use your scanner to survey planets
2. Look for resource deposits (Iron, Aluminum, Helium-3, etc.)
3. Find flat terrain for easier building
4. Consider proximity to other resources you'll need

### Basic Extractors

**Resource Extractors**: Place these on top of resource veins shown by your scanner. They automatically extract resources over time.

**Power Requirements**: Extractors need power. Build Solar Arrays or Wind Turbines depending on the planet's conditions. Check planet info for solar/wind efficiency.

**Storage**: Add Storage Containers to hold extracted resources. Link them to extractors using the linking system (LB/L1 button).

### Production Chains

You can create complex production chains:
- Extract Iron → Produce Iron into components
- Extract Helium-3 → Fuel production
- Multiple resources → Crafted items

Use the Industrial Workbench to produce higher-tier items from raw materials.

## Making Credits with Outposts

The most efficient credit-making outposts extract valuable resources and sell them.

**High-Value Resources**:
- Helium-3 (fuel, always in demand)
- Uranium (rare, high value)
- Aldumite (used in crafting)
- Iridium (rare material)

**Automation Strategy**:
1. Build extractors on high-value resource veins
2. Add ample storage (resources accumulate when you're away)
3. Visit periodically to collect and sell
4. Use Trade Authority vendors in major cities

**Cargo Link Network**: Advanced outposts can use Cargo Links to transfer resources between outposts automatically, but this requires significant investment.

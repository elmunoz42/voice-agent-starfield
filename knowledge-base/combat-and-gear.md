## Weapon Types

- Ballistic: conventional firearms (pistols, rifles, shotguns, snipers). Physical damage, good against most humans. Ammo is common.
- Laser: energy damage, effective against many targets and robots with fewer physical resistances.
- Particle beam: high damage energy weapons, often with slower fire.
- EM (electromagnetic): non-lethal damage that stuns and incapacitates. Good for pacifist runs and taking targets alive.
- Heavy weapons: grenade launchers, rocket launchers and miniguns. Free Lanes and Terran Armada added the Multi-Gun Platform (MGP), a modular heavy weapon.
- Melee: knives, blades and blunt weapons, boosted by Dueling and Martial Arts.
- Grenades and mines: thrown explosives, boosted by Demolitions.

Each weapon category has a matching Combat skill. Damage types face matching resistances: physical, energy and EM.

## Spacesuits, Helmets and Boost Packs

Your gear is a spacesuit, a helmet and a pack (boost pack). Gear gives physical, energy and EM resistance, environmental protection (thermal, airborne, corrosive, radiation) and sometimes carry weight or bonus effects.

Boost packs let you jump-jet. You need rank 1 of Boost Pack Training to use one, and different packs have different boost styles (vertical, forward and so on). Boost Assault Training adds combat bonuses.

In settlements you can set your helmet and suit to hide automatically, so your character wears outfits in breathable areas.

## Legendary Items, Quality Tiers and X-Tech

Gear comes in quality tiers. Legendary items roll special effects such as extra damage against certain enemies, explosive rounds, or effects while you are aiming or low on health.

Free Lanes (2026) expanded gear progression:
- Rank 4 legendary effects for weapons and suits.
- Additional quality tiers.
- Upgrade modules that improve one attribute of a weapon or suit.
- X-Tech, a new resource. Use it at workbenches to further customize legendary effects on weapons and armor, and at the Ship Optimization Terminal for ships.
- New weapon skins that you apply at a workbench.

X-Tech comes from Terran Armada Incursions, the X-Tech Extractor outpost module and other new content.

## Weapon and Armor Mods (Workbenches)

Weapon Workbench: install weapon mods such as scopes, barrels, magazines, muzzle devices and receivers (for example rapid or hair-trigger). You must research mods first at a Research Lab, and you need ranks in Weapon Engineering for advanced mods.

Spacesuit Workbench: upgrade suits, helmets and packs with mods. Requires research and the Spacesuit Design skill.

Workbenches are found in your ship (with the right habs), at outposts, in cities and at The Lodge. Mod crafting uses resources like iron, aluminum, adhesive, polymer and rarer materials.

## Ground Combat Tips

- Use cover. Enemies flank, and shields and resistances matter more than raw health.
- Med packs heal over time. Keep a stock and bind them to Favorites for quick access.
- Mix damage types: carry a ballistic weapon and an energy weapon so you can switch against high-resistance enemies.
- Boost packs let you reach high ground and dodge. Combining zero-g and low-gravity planets with the boost pack makes for great mobility.
- In zero-g areas (ship interiors without gravity, space stations), recoil and boosting move you around.
- Robots and turrets are vulnerable to Robotics skill bonuses and EM damage. Terran Armada enemies are mostly robots.
- Terrormorphs are deadly alien predators that can make humans turn on each other. Fight them at range.

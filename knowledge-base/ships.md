## Ship Basics

Your ship is your mobile base. It carries cargo, crew and passengers, and handles space travel and space combat. You get your first ship, the Frontier, early in the main quest. You can own many ships (set one as your Home Ship at a Ship Services Technician). Ships are bought from shipyards and ship vendors, earned from quests, or captured by boarding.

Ship classes: Class A ships are small and agile, Class B medium and Class C large and heavy. Flying Class B and Class C ships needs higher ranks of the Piloting skill. Class depends on the reactor installed.

Key stats: hull, shields, cargo capacity, crew capacity, fuel and grav jump range, mobility (agility), top speed and the reactor's power output.

## Ship Builder and Modules

Open the ship builder at any Ship Services Technician (at every spaceport) or at a shipyard. Modules include the cockpit, reactor, grav drive, fuel tanks, engines, shield generator, weapons, cargo holds (including shielded cargo for smuggling), habs (crew stations, workshops, infirmary, armory), landing gear, a docker (needed to dock with stations and board ships) and a landing bay.

Rules: all modules must be attached, the ship needs a cockpit, reactor, grav drive, fuel tank, engines, landing gear, docker and landing bay, and it must stay within the class limits. More mass lowers mobility, so balance engines against weight.

Starship Design skill ranks unlock better module tiers. Different manufacturers sell different parts. Parts vendors include shipyards in New Atlantis, Deimos Staryard (orbiting Mars), Taiyo Astroneering and Stroud-Eklund in Neon, and Hopetech in Hopetown.

Ship Decoration Mode (2024): decorate your ship's interior with furniture and clutter, much like an outpost.

## Space Combat Tips

Power: your reactor's power is split between weapons, shields, engines and the grav drive. Shift power into shields when you are under fire, and into weapons when you are attacking.

Weapon types: lasers are strong against shields, ballistics are strong against hull, particle beams are good against both, missiles deal heavy burst damage and EM weapons damage systems without destroying the ship (good for boarding). Turrets (automated weapons) fire on their own.

Targeting mode (Targeting Control Systems skill): lock onto an enemy and aim at specific systems such as engines, weapons, shields or the grav drive.

Boarding: disable the enemy's engines (EM weapons help), dock with it, clear the crew and take the cockpit. Register a captured ship at a Ship Services Technician for a fee before you can modify or sell it.

Repairs: Ship Parts repair hull during combat. Starship Engineering helps. Crew assigned to ship systems with matching skills add bonuses.

Stealth module (Free Lanes): lets ships cloak while boosting, which helps you reposition when fighting several enemies.

## Grav Jumps, Fuel and Cruise Mode

You travel between star systems by grav jumping from the starmap. Range depends on your grav drive and the Astrodynamics skill. Fuel tanks hold helium-3 for jumps. Long trips may need several jumps.

Cruise mode (Free Lanes 2026): you can fly through open space between planets in a system and stop at points of interest along the way. It is also useful for escaping Terran Armada Incursions, which block grav jumping in an area.

Contraband scanning: when you arrive at UC or Freestar planets, security may scan your ship. Shielded cargo modules and the Deception skill help you hide contraband. A failed scan means confiscation and a fine.

## Notable Ships and How to Get Them

- Frontier: the starting ship from the main quest, given to you by Barrett.
- Razorleaf: from the side quest The Mantis, a strong early ship.
- Star Eagle: reward for finishing the Freestar Rangers questline (The Hammer Falls).
- Crimson Fleet and UC SysDef questlines also reward gear and ships.
- Starborn Guardian: a powerful ship you start with in New Game Plus.
- Captured ships: Spacers, Ecliptic and Crimson Fleet ships can be boarded and stolen, then registered.

Free Lanes (2026) added a buildable Ship Optimization Terminal where you spend X-Tech to improve ship attributes, plus ship equipment schematics that, once discovered, unlock new equipment in the ship builder.

## Crew and Ship Stations

Ships have a crew capacity set by the cockpit and habs. Your active crew limit is also capped by the Ship Command skill. Crew with ship skills (for example Shield Systems, Ballistic Weapon Systems or Piloting) improve those systems while assigned to your ship. Crew can also be assigned to outposts.

Companions follow you on missions, and other crew stay on the ship or at outposts. Assign crew from the Crew menu at the ship's console or through dialogue.

## Free Lanes Update (Free, April 2026)

Free Lanes (update 1.16.236) launched April 7, 2026 alongside the PS5 release and the Terran Armada DLC. It is free for all players. Main additions:

- Cruise mode: fly through open space between planets in a system and stop at points of interest along the way.
- X-Tech: a new resource for further customizing legendary effects on weapons and armor, and for improving ship attributes at a buildable Ship Optimization Terminal.
- Rank 4 legendary effects, more quality tiers, and upgrade modules that improve one attribute of a weapon or suit.
- Ship equipment schematics: discover them to unlock new ship builder equipment. Also a stealth module that cloaks your ship while boosting.
- Legendary enemy modifiers, with a Gameplay Options setting for when they appear.
- Quantum Entanglement Device: carry some items through the Unity into New Game Plus.
- Starborn powers upgradable with Quantum Essence.
- Database menu tracking locations, resources and recipes.
- Moon Jumper vehicle, the Château des Étoiles player house and Colony War Action Hero collectibles that give bonuses and can be shown in display cases.
- Outposts: Shared Outpost Container, production rate info and precise placement.
- Anchorpoint Station in the Algorab system, plus new dungeons and encounters.
- Muria Siarkiewicz as a new crew member, and better loot in Expert and Master locked containers.

## Terran Armada DLC: Overview and How to Start

Terran Armada is the second paid expansion ($9.99, included with the Premium Edition), released April 7, 2026. Unlike Shattered Space, it spreads across the Settled Systems instead of one planet.

The Terran Armada is a breakaway military faction of former Freestar and UC soldiers who vanished during the Colony War. They claim to be Earth's true children and want to unify the Settled Systems by force, using advanced combat robots and technology that stops ships from grav jumping.

How to start: once you've reached Akila City in the main story, an SSNN news broadcast about the hijacked luxury cruise ship Opulence of the Stars plays at terminals and beacons in settlements. This starts Attack of the Terran Armada, which sends you to the Nirvana system to find a ship called Olokun orbiting Nirvana I. You don't need to finish the main quest.

Recommended level: Nirvana is a level 40 system, and guides suggest level 50 or higher, with strong shields and anti-robot weapons.

## Terran Armada: Incursions and Tips

Incursions are Terran Armada operations across the Settled Systems: hostile ships, ground forces and objectives, ranging from skirmishes to large battles. Some are tied to the story and others appear as you explore. They show on the starmap.

Inside an Incursion area, grav jumping and fast travel may be blocked. Defeat the threat or leave the area, and cruise mode is a good way to escape. The block only covers the affected area, not the whole system. You can lower how often optional Incursions appear in settings.

Rewards: equipment, X-Tech and materials.

Tips:
- Space: a strong shield generator, and weapons that hurt both shields and hull. Particle beams are recommended.
- Ground: lots of ammo, healing and anti-robot gear. The Robotics skill and EM damage help.
- New gear: the Multi-Gun Platform (MGP) modular heavy weapon and NASA-style tactical military gear.
- Save manually before big Delta and New Babylon decisions.

## Terran Armada Story Quests and Side Stories

Quest flow: Attack of the Terran Armada (find the Olokun at Nirvana I) → Lost Luxury (investigate the Opulence of the Stars, meet the robot Delta and the Terran Armada search party led by Yemoja) → Into the VOID (meet roboticist Roxanne Bourreau, connected to Delta's creation).

New Babylon side story: talk to Bernhard near the bar at Anchorpoint Station. It involves Caruso and Ivica (achievements Master of Magnetism and Stay of Execution).

The Arbitrator: assassination contracts, separate from Trackers Alliance bounties. Ten targets earn the Contract Killer achievement.

Faction outpost missions (Favors for Favors, Give and Take, Quid Pro Quo) unlock after main-story progress.

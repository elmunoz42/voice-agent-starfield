## Joining Constellation

Constellation is an organization of explorers seeking to understand mysterious artifacts scattered across the galaxy. You'll join them early in the main questline.

**First Steps**:
- Travel to New Atlantis on Jemison
- Go to Constellation Lodge in the MAST district
- Meet Sarah Morgan and other members
- Receive your first assignment

Constellation provides access to resources, ship upgrades, and a base of operations as you explore the Settled Systems.

<!-- spoiler: main-quest -->
## The Artifact Hunt

The main Constellation questline revolves around collecting mysterious artifacts.

**Artifact Locations**: Artifacts are found on various planets across multiple star systems. Each artifact emits a unique energy signature that Constellation can track.

**Visions and Powers**: When you approach artifacts, you'll experience strange visions. Collecting artifacts grants access to special abilities later in the questline.

**Key Questline Missions**:
1. "The Old Neighborhood" - Return to your roots
2. "Into the Unknown" - First major artifact discovery
3. "All That Money Can Buy" - Deal with a rival organization
4. "Starborn" - Major revelations about the artifacts

**The Hunter and the Emissary**: You'll encounter other beings called Starborn who are also seeking artifacts. Their motives vary.

<!-- spoiler: endgame -->
## Unity and New Game Plus

The ultimate goal is reaching Unity, which requires collecting all artifacts.

**What is Unity**: Unity is a gateway to another universe. Entering Unity allows you to become Starborn and start a New Game Plus cycle.

**New Game Plus Features**:
- Keep your skills and abilities
- Start with a Starborn ship
- Experience alternate dialogue and scenarios
- Collect artifacts again with variations
- Access to Starborn Guardian armor and ships

**Multiple Playthroughs**: Each NG+ cycle can be different. Some players choose different paths or make different choices to see alternate outcomes.

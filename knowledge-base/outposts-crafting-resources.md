## Outpost Basics

Outposts are bases you build on planets and moons. Start by placing an Outpost Beacon from the scanner (hand scanner, then the Outpost Beacon option) on a spot with resources you want. Your beacon's build area shows which resources you can extract there.

Core modules:
- Extractors pull resources (iron, aluminum, water, helium-3 and so on) from the ground. They need power.
- Power: solar arrays (better near bright stars), wind turbines (on windy planets), and fueled generators.
- Storage: solid, liquid and gas containers, matched to resource type.
- Habs and crew stations let you assign crew.
- Landing pad with ship builder, so you can modify ships at your outpost.
- Defenses: turrets protect against creatures and raiders.

You start with a limit of a few outposts, which the Planetary Habitation skill raises. Outpost Engineering unlocks more modules through research.

## Outpost Logistics: Cargo Links and Shared Containers

Output links connect extractors and storage at the same outpost. Cargo Links move resources between outposts in the same star system. Inter-system Cargo Links connect outposts in different systems and need helium-3 fuel.

Free Lanes (2026) added a Shared Outpost Container that gives access to inventory across multiple outposts, production rate information and a more precise placement mode.

Tips:
- Pick spots where several resources overlap (use the scanner).
- Put crew with Outpost Management or relevant resource skills at the outpost to boost output.
- Build a few defensive turrets on planets with hostile wildlife.
- Outposts are mainly for crafting materials and research, not for big money.

## Faction Outpost Missions (Free Lanes / Terran Armada)

After progressing through the main storyline, faction outpost missions become available: Favors for Favors, Give and Take, and Quid Pro Quo. You build an outpost to a faction's specification and hand it over. Requirements include an X-Tech Extractor, four defensive turrets and enough power to run the installation. The achievement This for That is tied to completing a faction handover.

You cannot freely donate any existing outpost for passive income. These are specific construction missions.

Free Lanes also added a pre-built elevated cabin (a furnished, customizable living module for outposts) and new decor such as faction-themed workbenches and small animal habitats.

## Research Lab and Crafting Stations

Research Lab: research projects in five categories (Pharmacology, Food and Drink, Outpost Development, Equipment and Weaponry) to unlock recipes, outpost modules and mods. Projects cost resources. Research Methods reduces costs.

Crafting stations:
- Industrial Workbench: craft manufactured components (adhesive, polymer, isocentered magnets and so on).
- Weapon Workbench: weapon mods.
- Spacesuit Workbench: suit, helmet and pack mods.
- Pharmaceutical Lab: med packs, chems and aid items (Chemistry and Medicine help).
- Cooking Station: food and drink (Gastronomy).

Special Projects (Master Science) allows experimental research and crafting of manufactured components such as advanced electronics.

## Resources and Surveying

Resources are inorganic (minerals and gases such as iron, aluminum, copper, nickel, cobalt, lithium, helium-3, tungsten) or organic (from plants and creatures, such as fiber, sealant, adhesive components, sedatives and spices). Rarer resources appear in higher level systems.

Surveying: scan planets from orbit to see resources and traits. On the surface, scan flora, fauna, resources and planetary traits with the hand scanner. A 100% survey yields valuable survey data you can sell. Surveying, Scanning, Botany, Zoology, Geology, Astrophysics and Astrodynamics help.

Planetary traits are unique features such as cave networks or ecological events. Discovering them counts toward survey completion.

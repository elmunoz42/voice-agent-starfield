## The Settled Systems: Setting and History

The Settled Systems are the human-colonized star systems near Sol. Humanity left Earth after its magnetosphere failed and the planet became uninhabitable. The grav drive made interstellar travel possible.

Major powers: the United Colonies (UC), a large republic based in New Atlantis, and the Freestar Collective, a looser frontier confederation of three systems based in Akila City. They fought the Colony War, which ended about a decade before the game's events. House Va'ruun, a religious society that worships the Great Serpent, keeps to itself and once fought humanity in the Serpent's Crusade.

Corporations such as Ryujin Industries, Stroud-Eklund, Nova Galactic, Taiyo Astroneering, Hopetech, Deimos and GalBank shape daily life. Pirates (the Crimson Fleet), mercenaries (Ecliptic) and bandits (Spacers) roam space.

The game begins in 2330.

## New Atlantis (Jemison, Alpha Centauri)

New Atlantis on Jemison is the capital of the United Colonies and the largest city. Districts:
- Spaceport: landing pad, Ship Services Technician, Trade Authority access nearby, and the Jemison Mercantile store.
- MAST district: UC government buildings and the UC Vanguard recruitment (Commander Tuala at the MAST building).
- Commercial district: shops, Enhance! (appearance changes), Centaur Arsenal (weapons), and The Viewport bar.
- Residential district: apartments, and The Lodge, Constellation's headquarters.
- The Well: the poorer lower city reached by elevator, with cheap apartments and side quests.
- Sanctum Universum and House of the Enlightened: religious sites.

GalBank, Trade Authority and the Lodge give many early side quests.

## Akila City, Neon and Cydonia

Akila City (Akila, Cheyenne system): the capital of the Freestar Collective. A walled frontier town surrounded by dangerous Ashta creatures. The Freestar Rangers are based here (Rangers' Hall). The Hitching Post spaceport bar has hireable crew.

Neon (Volii Alpha, Volii system): a floating pleasure city built on an ocean platform, run by Ryujin Industries and other corporations. The drug Aurora is legal here. Highlights: Ryujin Tower (join Ryujin Industries), the Astral Lounge club, Ebbside (the poor lower district with the Ebbside Strikers gang) and Taiyo Astroneering and Stroud-Eklund ship dealers.

Cydonia (Mars, Sol system): an underground mining colony. The Broken Spear bar has recruitable crew. Mars's moon Deimos hosts the Deimos Staryard.

## Other Notable Locations

- The Key (Kryx system): the Crimson Fleet's pirate space station.
- The Den (Wolf system): a space station hub for traders and smugglers, with Trade Authority.
- Hopetown (Polvo, Valo system): the Hopetech corporate town that builds ships.
- Paradiso (Porrima III): a resort.
- Red Mile (Porrima II): a dangerous bar where you can try running the creature-infested Mile.
- Vectera (moon of Anselon, Narion system): the Argos Extractors mine where the game begins.
- Va'ruun'kai: the House Va'ruun home world with the city of Dazra (Shattered Space expansion).
- Anchorpoint Station (Algorab system): a new point of interest added by Free Lanes, and a hub for Terran Armada content.
- Nirvana system: the starting area for the Terran Armada DLC (a level 40 system).

## System Levels and Exploration

Each star system has a level shown on the starmap. Enemies and loot scale with it, so higher numbers mean more danger. Core systems like Alpha Centauri, Sol and Cheyenne are low level. Outer systems go much higher.

Exploration tips:
- Land on planets near points of interest, or pick a random landing site for procedural locations such as abandoned mines, labs and outposts.
- Since 2024, surface maps are much more detailed.
- Free Lanes adjusted how points of interest are distributed on planets and their cooldown timers, and added new dungeons and space encounters.
- Random space encounters can include traders, distress calls, pirates and (with Terran Armada) Incursions.

## Shattered Space: Overview and How to Start

Shattered Space is Starfield's first story expansion, released September 30, 2024. It takes place on Va'ruun'kai, the home world of House Va'ruun, centered on the city of Dazra (Kavnyk system). Recommended level is 35 or higher.

Requirement: finish the first main mission, One Small Step.

How to start: open the starmap and travel to any system, selecting the system itself rather than a planet or moon. You'll get a distress signal from a damaged space station called the Oracle, which starts the quest What Remains. If it doesn't trigger, try a system where you have no other active objectives.

Rewards include a vacant house in Dazra, the Keep Armory and House Va'ruun weapons and gear such as the Va'ruun Schimaz, Va'ruun Penumbra and Va'ruun Starstorm.

## Shattered Space Quest List

House Va'ruun main questline:
1. What Remains: investigate the Oracle station.
2. The Promised, Broken: travel to Dazra on Va'ruun'kai.
3. Aligning the Houses: choose which Great House to support.
4. Conflict in Conviction
5. Zealous Overreach
6. Exhuming the Past
7. The Other Side
8. The Scaled Citadel: the finale.

Side quest example: Lost and Found. Talk to Vassilis Voria at the Halls of Healing in Dazra, who asks you to check on elderly Va'ruun.

Dazra has its own shops, a Va'ruun culture and lore about the Great Serpent. Andreja has extra dialogue about her people.

## The Promised, Broken Walkthrough Notes

After What Remains, fly down to Va'ruun'kai and reach Dazra. Follow Malibor Dul'kehf, who leads you to Inaza Kaisir. Walk the Serpent's Path to the marked statues and wait for Inaza's ritual dialogue to finish before the markers advance. For the final trial, pick two of the four effigies (Devotion, Obedience, Strength, Perseverance) and place them on the altars. Then take the elevator to the Mourning Ship Cockpit to find Malibor, return to Dazra, go past the giant statue and tell Ekris to begin work.

## Shattered Space Story and Endings

Dazra was devastated by a catastrophe tied to the Scaled Citadel, which left the city surrounded by dangerous anomalies and phantoms. You work with the Great Houses of Va'ruun to uncover what happened, and you choose which house to align with in Aligning the Houses.

Endings:
- Good ending: oppose a new Serpent's Crusade and lead your chosen House toward peace rather than war.
- Submitting to the antagonist Anasko is a game-over path. Releasing the Phantoms instead of ending life pod support leads there. Fighting him at the final decision is the correct answer.
- Leaving Va'ruun'kai early gives a disappointing ending where you wake up with the House leaders fussing over you.
- Insulting all three Houses gets you permanently expelled from Va'ruun'kai.

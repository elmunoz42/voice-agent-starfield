## Character Creation Overview

At the start you choose a face and body, a Background (which gives three starting skills at rank 1) and up to three Traits (each with an upside and a downside). You can change your appearance later at Enhance! clinics, for example in New Atlantis and Neon. Your background affects some dialogue but not the main story.

You do not need to pick traits. Three can make the game more interesting, but some have real costs. Some traits can be removed through in-game actions, for example by paying off the Dream Home mortgage.

## Traits: Factional and Religious

- United Colonies Native: special UC dialogue and better rewards from some UC missions. Crime bounties from other factions are greatly increased.
- Freestar Collective Settler: special Freestar dialogue and better rewards from some Freestar missions. Crime bounties from other factions are greatly increased.
- Neon Street Rat: special Neon dialogue and better rewards from some Neon missions. Crime bounties from other factions are greatly increased.
These three are mutually exclusive.

- Raised Universal: access to a chest of items at the Sanctum Universum in New Atlantis, plus special dialogue. You lose access to the House of the Enlightened chest.
- Raised Enlightened: access to a chest of items at the House of the Enlightened in New Atlantis. You lose access to the Sanctum Universum chest.
- Serpent's Embrace: grav jumping gives a temporary health and oxygen boost, but your health and oxygen drop if you don't jump regularly (like an addiction).
These three are mutually exclusive.

## Traits: Lifestyle and Combat

- Alien DNA: increased health and oxygen, but healing and food items are less effective.
- Dream Home: you own a customizable house on a peaceful planet (Nesoi), but it comes with a 125,000 credit GalBank mortgage paid weekly.
- Empath: actions your companion likes give a temporary combat boost. Actions they dislike give a temporary penalty.
- Extrovert: less oxygen used when adventuring with human companions, more when alone. Excludes Introvert.
- Introvert: less oxygen used when alone, more with human companions. Excludes Extrovert.
- Hero Worshipped: an Adoring Fan shows up, joins your crew and gives gifts, but talks constantly.
- Kid Stuff: your parents are alive and you can visit them (they live in New Atlantis), but 2% of your credits are sent to them weekly.
- Spaced: more health and oxygen in space, less on planet surfaces. Excludes Terra Firma.
- Terra Firma: more health and oxygen on the surface, less in space. Excludes Spaced.
- Taskmaster: crew-trained ship systems sometimes auto-repair to full when below 50%, but crew cost twice as much to hire.
- Wanted: extra damage when your health is low, but armed mercenaries occasionally show up to kill you.

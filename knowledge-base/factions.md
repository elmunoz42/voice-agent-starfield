## Joinable Factions Overview

All major faction questlines can be completed in one playthrough. They are independent of each other except for minor reputation effects.

- Constellation: explorers studying the Artifacts (the main quest).
- UC Vanguard: the United Colonies' volunteer defense force. Join by talking to Commander Tuala at the MAST in New Atlantis. 9 quests, from Supra et Ultra to A Legacy Forged.
- Freestar Rangers: the Freestar Collective's lawmen. Start in Akila City, where a bank robbery and hostage situation (Job Gone Wrong) leads to the Rangers. 8 quests, from Job Gone Wrong to The Hammer Falls.
- Ryujin Industries: a corporation based in Ryujin Tower, Neon. Apply for a job in the lobby. 14 quests, from Back to the Grind to Executive Level.
- Crimson Fleet: space pirates based at The Key. You either join as a UC SysDef undercover agent or directly. 8 quests, from Deep Cover to Legacy's End.
- House Va'ruun (Shattered Space): the Serpent's people on Va'ruun'kai. 8 quests.
- Ebbside Strikers (Neon): a small gang questline (The Audition, Display of Power, The Showdown).
- Trackers Alliance: bounty hunters (added as a free Creation).

Suggested order: UC Vanguard, Freestar Rangers, Ryujin, then Crimson Fleet, since the Fleet's choices change the most.

## Hostile Factions and Groups

- Spacers: lawless bandits and pirates found all over the frontier. Hostile on sight.
- Ecliptic: mercenaries for hire. Usually hostile.
- Crimson Fleet: pirates. Hostile unless you have allied with them.
- The First: a Freestar extremist group that opposes the Collective's government. Enemies in the Freestar Rangers questline.
- House Va'ruun zealots: Va'ruun ships sometimes appear as aggressive religious zealots.
- Terrormorphs: alien predators that devastated colonies. A focus of the UC Vanguard questline.
- Terran Armada (2026 DLC): a robotic military faction of former UC and Freestar soldiers who vanished during the Colony War.

Religions: Sanctum Universum (faith in a cosmic creator), the Enlightened (secular humanists) and the House Va'ruun worship of the Great Serpent.

## UC Vanguard Questline

Quest order: Supra et Ultra (orientation and exam) → Grunt Work (a Terrormorph test) → Delivering Devils (helping Percival out of debt) → Eyewitness (Terrormorphs at the spaceport) → Friends Like These (gain the approval of three factions for a plan to fight the Terrormorphs) → The Devils You Know (finding Reginald Orlasse) → War Relics (obtaining a Microcell) → Hostile Intelligence (collecting Terrormorph samples on Niira) → A Legacy Forged (the finale).

The story focuses on the Terrormorph threat and a long-dormant weapon to fight them. Key characters: Commander Tuala and Major Hadrian Sanon (recruitable as a companion after A Legacy Forged; find her at the TMO headquarters near Cydonia).

Rewards: First-Class Citizenship and a free penthouse apartment in Mercury Tower, New Atlantis, plus the UC Vanguard spacesuit, helmet, pilot pack and ship weapons.

## Freestar Rangers Questline

Quest order: Job Gone Wrong → Deputized → Where Hope Is Built → Shadows in Neon → Surgical Strike → On the Run → First to Fight, First to Die → The Hammer Falls.

The story begins with a bank robbery and hostage situation in Akila City and grows into a conspiracy involving The First, a Freestar extremist group led by Paxton Hull. In First to Fight, First to Die you go to Arcturus II and fight The First, and you can steal one of their ships there.

The Hammer Falls: evidence points to Ron Hope, Freestar governor and Hopetech CEO, as the one behind the conspiracy. You can arrest him, kill him (his security joins the fight) or take his bribe (20,000 credits, or 50,000 if you negotiate).

Rewards: credits (about 12,400 on the last quest), a special long-range rifle and the Star Eagle ship.

## Ryujin Industries Questline

Quest order: Back to the Grind → One Step Ahead → A New Narrative → Access is Key → Sowing Discord → Accidents Happen → Maintaining the Edge → Top Secrets → Background Checks → Guilty Parties → The Key Ingredient → Sabotage → Executive Level, plus the follow-up The Power of Persuasion.

You start as a low-level employee in Neon's Ryujin Tower and become a corporate operative for Ularu Chen, running stealth and espionage missions.

Sabotage: break into Infinity Ltd. You can try to save the innocent assistant Imogene. Executive Level: a board meeting decides whether Ryujin acquires Infinity and continues the Internal Neuroamp project. Depending on who you side with, either CEO Masako Imada or Ularu Chen gets ousted, and the votes shape Ryujin's future.

Rewards are mostly the same either way: the Neuroamp implant (which gives the Manipulation ability), the Ember weapon, Operative suit and helmet, a CQB-X and credits.

## Crimson Fleet Questline

Quest order: Deep Cover → Rook Meets King → Echoes of the Past → Breaking the Bank → The Best There Is → Absolute Power → Eye of the Storm → Legacy's End.

You usually start after being arrested by UC SysDef, who send you to infiltrate the Crimson Fleet at The Key as a double agent. You meet Fleet leader Delgado and the Fleet's captains, and work toward finding the legendary pirate treasure, the Legacy.

Key choice: in Eye of the Storm and Legacy's End you either betray the Fleet to UC SysDef or side with the pirates. Siding with the Fleet loses the UC SysDef mission board but makes Crimson Fleet outposts and ships friendly. Siding with SysDef means helping the UC take down the Fleet's leadership. Former pirate Austin Rake can be killed or spared with a faked death.

Rewards: about 250,000 credits either way, plus Pirate Swashbuckler gear and the Keelhauler and Revenant. Many players say the Fleet path's rewards are better.

## Constellation Members and the Lodge

Constellation is a small group of explorers based at The Lodge in New Atlantis. Members include Sarah Morgan (leader, UC veteran), Sam Coe (Freestar cowboy, with his daughter Cora), Barrett (cheerful, philosophical explorer), Andreja (a former House Va'ruun member and stealth specialist), Walter Stroud (the group's wealthy patron), Matteo Khatri and Noel (a young scientist). Vladimir Sall at the Eleos Retreat is an older member and an expert on the temples.

Vasco is the group's loyal robot, assigned to you with the Frontier.

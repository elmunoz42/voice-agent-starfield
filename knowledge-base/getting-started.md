## Starfield Overview

Starfield is a single-player space RPG by Bethesda Game Studios, released September 2023 on Xbox Series X|S and PC, and on PlayStation 5 on April 7, 2026. It is set in the year 2330 in the Settled Systems, a region of space roughly 50 light years around Earth's old neighborhood. Earth itself is uninhabitable after losing its magnetosphere. You play a miner who touches a mysterious Artifact and is recruited by Constellation, the last group of space explorers.

The game has more than 1,000 planets across over 100 star systems. Core activities are exploration, surveying planets, ship building, outpost building, faction questlines, crafting and combat both on foot and in space.

Expansions: Shattered Space (September 30, 2024, House Va'ruun story on Va'ruun'kai) and Terran Armada (April 7, 2026, robotic military faction spread across the Settled Systems). The free Free Lanes update arrived the same day as Terran Armada.

## First Hours: What to Do Early

1. Follow the main quest through One Small Step and The Old Neighborhood. This gets you to New Atlantis, Constellation, your first companions and a ship.
2. Put early skill points into high-value Novice skills: Boost Pack Training (required to use boost packs), Security (open more locks), Persuasion (talk your way out of fights), Commerce (better prices), Weight Lifting (carry more) or your main weapon skill.
3. Explore New Atlantis: the Spaceport, MAST district, the Commercial district and The Well (lower city). Pick up side quests from NPCs and mission boards.
4. Upgrade your ship's shields and weapons before taking hard space fights. Ship Services Technicians at spaceports handle repairs, upgrades and the ship builder.
5. Save often and keep several manual saves before big decisions.

Good early side quest: The Mantis. Find a slate on dead Spacers (often in early missions) that points you to a secret lair. The reward is the Mantis armor set and the Razorleaf ship, both strong for the early game.

## Survival Basics: Oxygen, Carry Weight and Afflictions

Oxygen (O2) drains as you sprint and use the boost pack. When it runs out, CO2 builds and you take damage. Rest, slow down, or put points into Fitness for more oxygen. The Introvert and Extrovert traits also change oxygen use.

Carry weight: going over your limit makes you encumbered. You use oxygen faster and your movement is impaired. Store items in your ship's cargo hold (reachable from the ship's inventory or the cargo terminal) and sell junk often. Weight Lifting raises your limit, and Payloads plus cargo modules grow ship storage.

Environmental hazards: planets have thermal, airborne, corrosive and radiation hazards. Spacesuits have environmental protection ratings. When protection runs out, you can catch afflictions such as burns, frostbite, lung damage, infections or radiation sickness. Treat them with aid items (bandages, antibiotics, and so on), at doctors in cities, or by sleeping. Environmental Conditioning and Decontamination help.

Sleeping in a bed gives the Rested bonus. Sleeping in a bed you own, or in your ship's bed with a companion, can give Well Rested, a bonus to XP for a while.

## Locks, Persuasion and Stealth Mechanics

Digipicking: locks are a concentric-ring puzzle solved with digipicks, which you can buy from general vendors. Lock difficulty is Novice, Advanced, Expert or Master. You need ranks in the Security skill to attempt the higher tiers. The Free Lanes update improved the loot in Expert and Master lock containers.

Persuasion: some dialogue starts a persuasion challenge. Options are color-coded by difficulty (green is easy, yellow medium, red hard), and each grants points toward a goal within a limited number of turns. The Persuasion skill makes it easier. Some backgrounds and traits unlock unique dialogue options.

Stealth: crouch to sneak. A stealth meter shows whether you are Hidden, Caution, Danger or Detected. Sneak attacks deal bonus damage. The Stealth and Concealment skills improve it, and Theft lets you pickpocket.

Crime: stealing, trespassing or assault in a faction's territory gives you a bounty with that faction (UC, Freestar, Ryujin and others). Pay it off, go to jail or talk your way out. Bounties for the UC can draw the attention of UC SysDef.

## Useful Settings and Menus

Difficulty: you can set overall difficulty and, since the 2024 updates, fine-tune individual settings such as damage dealt and taken, enemy health, carry capacity, vendor credits, ship part costs, oxygen, afflictions and more under Gameplay Options.

Surface maps: since 2024, planet surface maps show detailed terrain, points of interest and city layouts, which makes navigation much easier.

Database menu (Free Lanes, 2026): tracks the locations, resources and recipes you have discovered.

Land vehicles: the REV-8 rover (August 2024) can be bought from a ship technician and deployed from your ship on planet surfaces. Free Lanes added a second vehicle, the Moon Jumper.

Cruise mode (Free Lanes, 2026): fly freely through open space between planets in a system and stop at points of interest along the way, instead of only grav-jumping or fast-travelling.

Legendary Enemy Modifiers (Free Lanes): legendary enemies can spawn with extra modifiers. Gameplay Options controls when they start to appear.

## Money-Making Tips (Credits)

- Sell contraband (for example Aurora, harvested organs, untraceable items) to Trade Authority vendors. They are found in places like The Den, The Key and many settlements. UC and Freestar ships scan for contraband when you land in their space, so use shielded cargo modules.
- Steal or capture enemy ships by boarding them, then register them at a Ship Services Technician and sell them. This is a classic credits loop.
- Take mission board contracts at spaceports for cargo, passenger and bounty missions.
- Major faction questlines pay well. The Crimson Fleet questline ends with about 250,000 credits.
- Vendors have a limited credit pool that refreshes over time, so spread sales across several vendors. Commerce gives better prices.
- Survey planets completely (fauna, flora, resources and traits) and sell the survey data.
- Loot and sell good weapons and armor, especially legendary items.

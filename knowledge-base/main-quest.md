## Main Quest Overview (Spoiler-Free)

The main questline is the Constellation story. You start as a miner at Argos Extractors on Vectera. After touching a strange metal Artifact and seeing a vision, you are sent to Constellation in New Atlantis to learn what the Artifacts are. Along the way you collect more Artifacts, explore ancient temples and uncover a mystery tied to the origins of the Settled Systems.

The main quest has about 19 missions, from One Small Step to Revelation and the epilogue One Giant Leap. Some are grouped and can be done in any order. It is fine to take breaks for faction questlines and side content.

## Main Quest Order (Part 1)

1. One Small Step: the mine on Vectera, meeting Barrett, getting the Frontier and Vasco, and the first trip to New Atlantis.
2. The Old Neighborhood: Constellation, the Lodge and Sarah Morgan.
3. The Empty Nest and 4. Back to Vectera: chasing Artifacts with Sam and Barrett.
5. Into the Unknown: the first temple and your first power, Anti-Gravity Field.
6. All That Money Can Buy: bidding for an Artifact owned by a rich collector.
7. Starborn: a powerful masked figure appears.
8. Further Into the Unknown: more temples and powers.
9. Short Sighted and 10. No Sudden Moves: more Artifact hunting as the Starborn close in.

<!-- spoiler: main-quest -->
## Main Quest Order (Part 2)

11. High Price to Pay: a devastating attack on Constellation.
12. Unity: the group regroups and the Starborn's true nature is revealed.
13. In Their Footsteps, 14. Final Glimpses and 15. Entangled: searching for the last Artifacts. Entangled takes you to a research station split across two realities, where you can save Rafael Aguerro.
16. Missed Beyond Measure: a memorial for the lost.
17. Unearthed: reaching the Buried Temple on Masada III.
18. Revelation: the final confrontation and your choice.
19. One Giant Leap: the epilogue and entering the Unity.

Power From Beyond is a repeatable temple quest that lets you gather more powers.

## Starborn, the Hunter and the Emissary

The Starborn are people who have gathered all the Artifacts, passed through the Unity and come back across universes. Two Starborn rivals pursue the Artifacts: the Hunter and the Emissary.

- The Hunter believes the Artifacts' power belongs to whoever is strong enough to take it.
- The Emissary wants to control access to the Armillary, the device built from the Artifacts, to keep it from the undeserving.

Constellation builds an Armillary on your ship to track the Artifacts and to complete the gateway to the Unity.

## High Price to Pay: Who Dies

In High Price to Pay, the Hunter attacks Constellation in two places at once, the Lodge in New Atlantis and the Eye space station. You must choose which to defend. Whatever you choose, you cannot defeat the Hunter here, and one Constellation member dies. It is usually one of Sarah, Sam, Barrett or Andreja, depending on your choices and who is at each location. Sources disagree on the exact rules. Commonly, the companion you are traveling with survives, and the death happens at the location you did not defend.

The dead companion's Starborn counterpart returns later.

## Hunter and Emissary Identities

The Hunter is an alternate-universe version of Keeper Aquilus, the leader of the Sanctum Universum in New Atlantis.

The Emissary is an alternate-universe version of the companion who died in High Price to Pay.

## Endings: Revelation and the Unity

In Revelation, you confront the Hunter and the Emissary at the Buried Temple. You can side with the Hunter, side with the Emissary or reject both. Each leads to a different confrontation and a different epilogue for Constellation. Siding with the Hunter gives the darkest epilogue, siding with the Emissary is widely seen as the good ending, and you can also talk both down and stay independent.

After Revelation you speak with a parallel version of yourself and enter the Unity, a space between universes. You can then return to your current universe, or cross into a new universe as a Starborn, which starts New Game Plus. No matter what you choose, the story ends with you becoming Starborn.

See the Starborn powers and New Game Plus guide for details.

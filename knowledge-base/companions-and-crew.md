## Companions vs Crew

Companions travel with you, fight beside you, comment on your choices and have affinity with you. Crew are hires who boost your ship or outposts with their skills. Most named characters can be both: set one as your active companion and assign the rest to your ship or outposts.

Affinity: companions like or dislike your actions (for example, Sarah dislikes stealing, and Sam likes helping people). High affinity unlocks personal conversations, personal quests and romance. You can give companions gear and items, and give them orders to loot, open doors or attack targets.

Romance: the four core Constellation companions, Sarah Morgan, Sam Coe, Barrett and Andreja, can be romanced and eventually married through dialogue once affinity is high enough.

Crew limits depend on the Ship Command skill and your ship's crew capacity.

## Core Constellation Companions

- Sarah Morgan: leader of Constellation and a former UC Navy officer. Principled, she likes exploration and honesty and dislikes theft and cruelty. Skills: Astrodynamics, Lasers, Leadership, Botany. Available after The Old Neighborhood.
- Sam Coe: a Freestar cowboy from a prominent Akila family, who travels with his daughter Cora. Likes helping people and fair play. Skills: Piloting, Rifle Certification, Payloads, Geology. Available after The Empty Nest.
- Barrett: a warm, talkative, philosophical explorer. He gives you the Frontier at the start. Skills: Starship Engineering, Particle Beam Weapon Systems, Robotics, Gastronomy. Available after Back to Vectera.
- Andreja: a reserved former member of House Va'ruun. She approves of decisive and pragmatic choices. Skills: Stealth, Particle Beams, Energy Weapon Systems, Theft. Available after Into the Unknown.
- Vasco: Constellation's loyal robot, with you from One Small Step. Skills: Aneutronic Fusion, Shield Systems, EM Weapon Systems. He does not use or need oxygen.

## Recruitable Crew: Where to Find Them (Part 1)

- Andromeda Kepler: The Broken Spear bar, Cydonia (Mars).
- Moara Otero: The Broken Spear, Cydonia, after The Old Neighborhood.
- Omari Hassan: The Hitching Post, Akila City, for a small one-time fee.
- Rosie Tannehill: The Hitching Post, Akila City, for a small fee.
- Marika Boros, Gideon Aker and Simeon Bankowski: The Viewport bar, New Atlantis.
- Mickey Caviar: the Astral Lounge, Neon.
- Jessamine Griffin: The Last Nova bar on The Key (Crimson Fleet station).
- Betty Howser: in orbit of Heinlein I after a Crimson Fleet attack. Give her ship parts to repair her ship and she joins you.
- Heller: recruitable after The Empty Nest, when you return to Vectera.

## Recruitable Crew: Where to Find Them (Part 2)

- Amelia Earhart: tied to the side quest Operation Starseed (Charybdis system, the Crucible). Recruit her after finishing it.
- Major Hadrian Sanon: after the UC Vanguard finale A Legacy Forged, at TMO Headquarters near Cydonia.
- Autumn MacMillan: after the Freestar Rangers finale The Hammer Falls.
- Mathis Castillo: through the Crimson Fleet finale Legacy's End.
- Rafael Aguerro: can be saved during the main quest Entangled, then recruited.
- Matteo Khatri: a Constellation member at the Lodge who can join your crew.
- Muria Siarkiewicz (Free Lanes, 2026): a job applicant in the GalBank lobby in New Atlantis. Talk to her about her interview, wait about 5 to 6 in-game hours, then offer her a job (reported cost about 15,000 credits).
- Adoring Fan: only with the Hero Worshipped trait. He finds you randomly and insists on joining.

Generic crew: randomized unnamed hires appear in bars and ports. They usually specialize in one skill, and persuasion can lower their price.

## Terran Armada Companions (DLC)

Delta is a combat robot built with Terran Armada technology, met during the DLC quest Lost Luxury aboard the Opulence of the Stars. Lead developer Emil Pagliarulo described Delta as "not evil, but definitely not good." Delta travels with you during the DLC and becomes a regular crew member after you finish its storyline. Delta has its own upgrade system (achievement: Overclocked).

Roxanne Bourreau, the roboticist connected to Delta's creation, joins you temporarily in Into the VOID. She stays at Anchorpoint Station afterward and is not a permanent recruit.

## Major Updates Timeline

- 2023 (launch, September): base game on Xbox Series X|S and PC.
- Early to mid 2024: granular difficulty settings, detailed surface maps, better inventory management, Ship Decoration Mode, 60 FPS options on Xbox and many quality-of-life fixes.
- August 2024 (update 1.13.61): the REV-8 land vehicle (buy and unlock it at a ship technician), plus frame rate target and visuals vs performance settings.
- September 30, 2024: Shattered Space expansion.
- 2024 to 2025: Creations (official mods, free and paid), such as the Trackers Alliance bounty questlines.
- May 2025 (update 1.15.216): more Creations support, Very Low display settings and many quest and DLC fixes.
- April 7, 2026: PS5 release, the free Free Lanes update (1.16.236) and the Terran Armada DLC.

## Notable Side Quests

- The Mantis: find a slate on Spacers to start it. Puzzles in a hidden lair reward the Mantis armor and the Razorleaf ship. Great at low level.
- Operation Starseed: a cult and a famous clone in the Charybdis system (recruit Amelia Earhart afterward).
- First Contact: a generation colony ship that left Earth before the grav drive arrives at a planet that has since been settled.
- Ebbside Strikers questline in Neon: a short gang storyline with the Street Sweeper reward.
- Kid Stuff trait content: visit your parents in New Atlantis for unique scenes.
- Mission boards: radiant missions for cargo, passengers, bounties and surveying in every spaceport.
- Red Mile: run the Mile on Porrima II for credits and bragging rights.

Talking to NPCs in cities (especially New Atlantis, Akila City and Neon) and using the activity log will reveal many more.

## Player Homes

You can buy or earn homes:
- The Well apartment in New Atlantis (cheap, can be bought).
- Mercury Tower penthouse in New Atlantis (UC Vanguard reward).
- Dazra vacant house (Shattered Space).
- Château des Étoiles (added by Free Lanes, 2026).
- Dream Home on Nesoi (Dream Home trait, with a mortgage).
- More homes can be bought in other major cities.

Homes have storage, beds for the Rested bonus and display space. Outposts and ships can also act as homes.

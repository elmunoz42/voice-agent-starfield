## Ship Building Basics

Starfield's ship builder allows you to customize every aspect of your spacecraft. Access the ship builder at any spaceport's Ship Services technician.

### Key Components

**Hab Modules**: Living quarters and crew stations. Different habs provide different crew capacity and aesthetics. The Companionway and Docker are required to connect modules.

**Reactor**: Powers your ship. Higher class reactors (A, B, C) provide more power for weapons and engines. Your Piloting skill determines which class you can use.

**Grav Drive**: Required for interstellar jumps. Better grav drives have longer jump ranges and lower fuel consumption.

**Engines**: Provide mobility and speed. Place multiple engines for better performance, but watch your power budget.

**Weapons**: Lasers (good vs shields), ballistics (good vs hull), missiles (high damage, limited ammo). Mix weapon types for versatility.

### Building Tips

1. Start with the cockpit - it determines your ship's orientation
2. Add the reactor early to see your power budget
3. Balance mass and thrust - heavy ships are sluggish
4. Symmetry isn't required but helps with aesthetics
5. Class C ships need Piloting skill rank 4

## Ship Combat Strategies

Effective ship combat requires managing power distribution and using the right weapons.

**Power Management**: Divert power to weapons for offense, engines for mobility, or shields for defense. The default balanced allocation works for most encounters.

**Targeting Systems**: Lock onto enemy ships to see their hull and shield status. Target specific subsystems (engines, weapons, shields) to disable threats.

**Missile Usage**: Missiles deal heavy damage but have limited ammo. Save them for tough enemies or finish off low-health targets.

**Shield Phases**: Shields have different phases. If your weapons aren't damaging shields effectively, try switching weapon types.

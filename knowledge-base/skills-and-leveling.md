## How Skills and Leveling Work

You earn XP from quests, combat, discovering locations, surveying, crafting and persuasion. Each level grants one skill point. There is no level cap.

There are 82 skills in five trees: Physical, Social, Combat, Science and Tech. Each tree has four tiers: Novice, Advanced, Expert and Master. Higher tiers unlock once you have spent enough points in that tree (commonly cited as 4 points for Advanced, 8 for Expert and 12 for Master).

Each skill has 4 ranks. Spending a point gives rank 1. To unlock the next rank you must first complete that skill's challenge, which means using the skill (for example, kill enemies with pistols to rank up Pistol Certification). Track challenges on the Skills screen.

Tip: since ranks are gated by challenges, spread points across a few skills you actually use instead of dumping everything into one.

## Physical Skills

Novice: Boxing (stronger unarmed attacks), Fitness (more oxygen), Stealth (better sneaking and sneak attacks), Weight Lifting (more carry capacity), Wellness (more max health).

Advanced: Energy Weapon Dissipation (less energy damage taken), Environmental Conditioning (better environmental resistances), Gymnastics (better movement), Nutrition (food and drink more effective), Pain Tolerance (less physical damage taken).

Expert: Cellular Regeneration (recover from injuries), Decontamination (recover from infections), Martial Arts (better melee and unarmed).

Master: Concealment (stronger stealth and sneak attacks), Neurostrikes (unarmed attacks can stun), Rejuvenation (health regenerates).

## Social Skills

Novice: Commerce (better buy and sell prices), Gastronomy (food and drink crafting and research), Persuasion (better chance to convince), Scavenging (more credits, ammo and aid from loot), Theft (pickpocketing).

Advanced: Deception (better piracy and smuggling, helps hide contraband), Diplomacy (makes a target stop fighting for a while), Intimidation (makes a target flee), Isolation (more damage and resistance without companions), Negotiation (better bribery).

Expert: Instigation (makes a target attack its allies), Leadership (companion bonuses), Outpost Management (outpost bonuses, such as more crew at outposts).

Master: Manipulation (a target obeys your commands for a while), Ship Command (more active crew members and ship crew), Xenosociology (makes creatures flee, fight each other or obey).

## Combat Skills

Novice: Ballistics (ballistic weapon damage), Dueling (melee damage), Lasers (laser weapon damage), Pistol Certification (pistol damage), Shotgun Certification (shotgun damage).

Advanced: Demolitions (explosive damage and radius), Heavy Weapons Certification (heavy weapon damage), Incapacitation (EM weapon effectiveness), Particle Beams (particle beam damage), Rifle Certification (rifle damage).

Expert: Marksmanship (crits with non-automatic weapons), Rapid Reloading (faster reloads), Sniper Certification (scoped weapon aim and damage), Targeting (hip-fire accuracy and range).

Master: Armor Penetration (attacks ignore armor), Crippling (enemies more likely to be downed), Sharpshooting (more critical damage with ranged weapons).

## Science Skills

Novice: Astrodynamics (better grav jumps, less fuel), Geology (better inorganic resource harvesting), Medicine (healing items more effective), Research Methods (cheaper research and crafting), Surveying (better hand scanner range and zoom).

Advanced: Botany (organic harvesting from plants), Scanning (detect resources from orbit, ship information), Spacesuit Design (suit mod crafting and research), Weapon Engineering (weapon mod crafting and research), Zoology (harvesting from creatures and creature info).

Expert: Astrophysics (better planet and moon scanning), Chemistry (chem crafting and research), Outpost Engineering (outpost module building and research).

Master: Aneutronic Fusion (ship reactors produce extra power), Planetary Habitation (outposts on extreme planets and a higher outpost limit), Special Projects (experimental research and manufactured components).

## Tech Skills

Novice: Ballistic Weapon Systems (ship ballistic damage), Boost Pack Training (required to use a boost pack, and improves it), Piloting (ship handling; higher ranks are needed to fly Class B and Class C ships), Security (hack harder locks), Targeting Control Systems (ship targeting mode, so you can aim at specific ship systems).

Advanced: Energy Weapon Systems (ship laser damage), Engine Systems (ship speed and boost), Payloads (more ship cargo), Shield Systems (stronger ship shields).

Expert: Missile Weapon Systems (ship missiles), Particle Beam Weapon Systems (ship particle beams), Robotics (more damage to robots and turrets), Starship Design (unlocks better ship modules in the ship builder), Starship Engineering (ship repair and damage mitigation).

Master: Automated Weapon Systems (ship turrets), Boost Assault Training (combat bonuses with the boost pack), EM Weapon Systems (ship EM weapons, for disabling ships to board).

## Recommended Skill Priorities

Early must-haves: Boost Pack Training (mobility), Security (locks and loot), Persuasion (skip fights, better rewards), Piloting (to fly bigger ship classes later), and the Novice skill for your favorite weapon type.

For space combat: Targeting Control Systems (lock on and disable engines to board ships), Shield Systems, Ballistic or Energy Weapon Systems, and Starship Design for better modules.

For crafting and outposts: Research Methods, Weapon Engineering, Spacesuit Design, Outpost Engineering, Botany, Geology, Zoology and Special Projects.

Ship Command (Master Social) raises your active crew count, and Leadership improves companions.

The Ryujin Industries questline unlocks the Neuroamp, which grants the Manipulation ability without spending skill points.

Magazines found across the Settled Systems give small permanent bonuses. Collecting them is a good habit.
